Welcome to Barangay Bagacay

A peaceful rural community in Passi City where agriculture, nature, and unity shape everyday life. This barangay is known for its simple lifestyle, strong community cooperation, and dedication to growth and development.

Residents work together in farming and local activities while preserving traditions that reflect the identity and culture of the community. Despite modernization, the barangay continues to value harmony, simplicity, and collective progress.

This portal serves as a gateway for information, updates, and services that help strengthen communication and participation within the barangay.
