History

Passi City’s Barangay Bagacay is a rural farming community with a history closely connected to the growth of Passi City. Passi was founded during the Spanish period in 1584 and officially became a pueblo or town in 1766.

As one of the barangays of Passi, Bagacay developed as an agricultural settlement where families depended mainly on rice farming, sugarcane planting, and other local crops for livelihood.

The name “Bagacay” is believed to have come from a type of bamboo grass called bagacay, which commonly grows in the Visayas. Throughout the years, the barangay remained a peaceful rural community with strong family ties and agricultural traditions.

Vision

A peaceful, progressive, and self-reliant community where residents are united, responsible, and actively participate in promoting sustainable development, a clean and safe environment, and a better quality of life for present and future generations.

Mission

To provide effective and transparent leadership through good governance, promote community unity and agricultural development, maintain peace and order, protect and preserve the environment, and deliver programs and services that improve the social, economic, and overall well-being of every resident.
